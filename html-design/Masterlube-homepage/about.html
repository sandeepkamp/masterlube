About MasterLube

Built by Engineers Who Understand Equipment

Since 1985, MasterLube has designed and manufactured automatic lubrication systems that keep equipment running smoother, longer, and with less downtime.

Our Story

Decades of Lubrication Expertise

MasterLube started as a small machine shop solving a simple problem: equipment failing early because lubrication was inconsistent or forgotten entirely. Today, our automatic lubrication systems are trusted across construction, mining, agriculture, transportation, and specialty applications worldwide.

We design every system to be reliable in the field, easy to install, and simple to maintain — because the best lubrication system is one that just works.
